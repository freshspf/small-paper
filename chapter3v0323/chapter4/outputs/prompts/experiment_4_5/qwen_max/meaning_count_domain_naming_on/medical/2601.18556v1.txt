The proposed SDA-QEC (Simpliﬁed Di!usion Augmentation with Quantum-Enhanced
Classiﬁcation) framework addresses the prevalent class imbalance problem in medical im-

The overall workﬂow consists of four core stages: (1) data loading and class distri-
