VI. CONCLUSION
We presented a configuration-space collision reasoning and
trajectory optimization framework for whole-body mobile ma-
nipulation in dense, cluttered, and unbounded environments.
We introduced GCDF as a generalization of CDF that cango beyond purely rotational manipulators and consider mobile
manipulators with both translational and rotational joints.
We developed a one-time offline data collection and training
pipeline to learn an implicit neural GCDF with accurate values
and first-order information. Building on this representation,
we proposed a high-performance C++ sequential convex opti-
mization algorithm that natively supports neural implicit con-
straints through batched GPU queries, sparsity-aware active-
constraint selection, and online constraint injection. Extensive
randomized clutter benchmarks and real-world experiments
demonstrate robust, efficient, and non-conservative whole-
body motion generation, highlighting the benefit of enforcing
collision avoidance directly in configuration space.
