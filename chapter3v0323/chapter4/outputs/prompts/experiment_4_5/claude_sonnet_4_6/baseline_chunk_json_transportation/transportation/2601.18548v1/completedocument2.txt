Challenges.Unlike fixed-base manipulators, mobile manip-

often converging within only a few iterations.
Despite its computational efficiency, this paradigm suffers
from fundamental limitations in both stages. On the front-
end side, decoupling the base and arm search undermines
holistic whole-body coordination. In complex environments
where simultaneous coordination is required amid intricate
obstacle layouts, the front-end search can fail; this would cause
the entire pipeline to collapse since the back-end optimizer is
constrained to local refinements near the front-end solution.

optimizer cannot recover, despite its potential exploration
capability. On the back-end side, these methods typically rely

reduces collision constraints complexity, it creates a funda-

the sum of ESDF penalties over spheres, whereas the position
of each sphere is coupled through highly nonlinear forward
kinematics.
