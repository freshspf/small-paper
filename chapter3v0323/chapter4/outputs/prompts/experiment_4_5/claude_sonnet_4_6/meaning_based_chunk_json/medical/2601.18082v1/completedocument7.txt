VI. DISCUSSION
The present results show that FALQON and ITE-
FALQON can reliably identify safe or synergistic subsets
in small DDI networks, and the structure of real phar-
macological data suggests that the method can scale be-
yond the six-drug examples studied here. Large clinical
DDI databases such as DrugBank or SYNERGxDB con-
tainhundredsofdrugs, but the correspondinginteraction
graphs are highly sparse: only a small fraction of drug
pairs exhibit clinically documented interactions. This
