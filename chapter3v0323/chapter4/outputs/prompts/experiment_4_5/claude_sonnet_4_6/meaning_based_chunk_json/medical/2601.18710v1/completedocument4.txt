B. Scaling with Dataset Size
Quantum and quantum-inspired methods achieve perfor-
mance only 12–15% below classical CNN despite operating
on severely constrained data representations. EP improves
with more data, reaching 86.4% accuracy—only 12% be-

performance: maintaining 83% accuracy across all dataset
scales (50–250 samples/class, Fig. 1). This demonstrates VQC
extracts robust discriminative features even from minimal
training data—an advantage for medical domains with anno-
tation scarcity. In contrast, CNN requires 5×more data (250
vs. 50 samples) to reach peak performance, demonstrating
that quantum methods compete effectively when data is the
limiting resource rather than model capacity.
