In the Earth observation satellite, the main fac-
tors that affect image quality are sensor noise
and atmospheric distortions[45]. To conduct sim-
ulation experiments on sensor noise in satellites,
we follow [13] and introduce three noise models:
Gaussian noise, impulsive noise (salt-and-pepper
noise), and Poisson noise (shot noise). The Gaus-
sian noise simulation accounts for both thermal
and quantization disturbances[46]. This type of
noise is independent of the signal. Impulsive noise
refers to the circumstances in which the sen-
sor generates bad pixels during data acquisition.
These bad pixels encompass both non-responsive
black pixels, which fail to capture any data, and
saturated white pixels[47]. Poisson noise is used
to simulate signal-dependent photon noise due to
its inherent correlation with light measurement
processes[48].
