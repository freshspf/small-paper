Related Work
Recent research has explored LLMs for mental health de-
tection and screening tasks. Six studies in the current lit-erature focus specifically on detection and screening appli-
cations, particularly targeting depression, suicide risk, and
general mental health conditions (Guo et al. 2024b; Jin et al.
2025b; Yang et al. 2023; Xu et al. 2024; Omar et al. 2024).
These studies consistently report that LLMs outperform tra-
ditional neural network baselines, especially when enhanced
with techniques such as emotion-enhanced prompting or
fine-tuning approaches. Yang et al. (2023) demonstrated that
ChatGPT achieves superior performance among examined
LLMs, though it still underperforms compared to advanced
supervised methods specifically designed for mental health
tasks. Similarly, Omar et al. (2024) found that ChatGPT
improved F-score by up to 13.11% and Correlation Coeffi-
cient by up to 15.95% in Reddit post analysis, while GPT-3
achieved approximately 87% accuracy in classifying mental
health disorders. The application of LLMs as conversational
agents represents another significant research direction, with
five studies examining conversation or intervention applica-
tions (Gabriel et al. 2024; Marrapese et al. 2024; Guo et al.
2024b; Omar et al. 2024).
