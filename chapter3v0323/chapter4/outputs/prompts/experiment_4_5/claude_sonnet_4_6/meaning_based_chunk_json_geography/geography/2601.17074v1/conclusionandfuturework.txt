We presented PhysE-Inv, a physics-encoded inverse modeling framework designed to estimate time-

learning to achieve a surjective inversion strategy . PhysE-Inv accurately reconstructed the seasonal

estimation of unobserved variables globally. Future work will incorporate Bayesian uncertainty
quantification to validate learned parameters against independent remote-sensing and in-situ sources.
