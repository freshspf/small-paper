Several limitations are inherent to simulation-based decision
support. First, outcomes depend on modeling assumptions in
the hazard, transport, and valuation modules; results should
therefore be read as conditional on the modeled dynamics
rather than as forecasts. Second, our climate uncertainty

training remains computationally intensive, constraining the
