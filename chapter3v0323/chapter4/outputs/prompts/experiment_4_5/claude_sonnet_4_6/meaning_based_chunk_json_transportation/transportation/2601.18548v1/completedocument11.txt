In this work, we chooseCRISP[88] as our backbone solver
for its computational efficiency with CppAD-based automatic

we extend the solver with the following key modifications.
First, we enable GPU-parallel querying of implicit function
values and gradients during the solving process. The gradients
from parallel GCDF queries form a dense matrix inRNM×n.
Second, we design a compact representation for the structure
of GCDF constraints that enables online reconstruction of
constraint mappings and automatic derivation of their spar-
sity patterns. This representation captures the relationship
between robot query points, obstacle points, and configuration
variables, allowing efficient adaptation to varying obstacle
configurations. Third, we implement efficient memory-level
sparse mapping from the dense GCDF gradient matrix into
the overall problem’s sparse Jacobian matrix. This process,
combined with GPU-parallel querying, significantly improves
computational efficiency.
Implementation.To enable cross-platform GPU querying

compatibility, our solver dynamically loads this library during
the online phase, providing efficient inference functions for

integrated into the optimization loop. The implementation of
